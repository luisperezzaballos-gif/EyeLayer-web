A Virtual Eye that learns from human ocular tissue.

EyeLayer.Bio is developing OPSIS, a drug discovery platform for eye disease. It is designed to connect computational target prediction with ex vivo experiments in diseased human corneal tissue, so that each experiment improves the next prediction.

The problem

Many eye diseases have no treatment that addresses their molecular cause

Fuchs endothelial corneal dystrophy progressively destroys the cell layer that keeps the cornea clear. Advanced disease is treated by transplanting donor tissue. Finding drug targets is slowed by how little human molecular data exists for these tissues, and by preclinical models that do not reproduce them well.

The platform

OPSIS: two parts of one discovery engine

Discovery proposes. Validation tests. Neither is a separate product.

OPSIS Discovery

The computational engine

Aims to reconstruct disease biology from human tissue and rank therapeutic targets.

  • Spatial transcriptomics and proteomics that keep each signal in its place in the tissue
  • Genetic and clinical information from the same patients
  • A Virtual Cornea: a computational model linking molecular changes to cells and regions
  • Output: a short list of candidate targets, each one a hypothesis
OPSIS Validation

The experimental component

Aims to test those candidates ex vivo in diseased human corneal tissue and measure the response.

  • Ex vivo human corneal tissue, kept alive outside the body
  • Diseased tissue obtained from patients undergoing corneal surgery
  • Target-modulated and control conditions compared side by side
  • Output: evidence of biological relevance, returned to the model
The learning loop

Every experiment is designed to refine the next prediction

A conventional AI platform analyses data that already exists. OPSIS is designed to generate new experimental evidence in human tissue and feed it back into the model.

01 · DISCOVERY

Predict

The model nominates and ranks candidate targets.

02 · VALIDATION

Test

Candidates are modulated ex vivo in diseased human corneal tissue.

03 · VALIDATION

Measure

Tissue responses are recorded against controls.

04 · DISCOVERY

Learn

Results update the networks and reprioritise the targets.

and predict again
First focus and roadmap

Start with one tissue, one disease

Now · concept and planning

Fuchs endothelial corneal dystrophy

The human corneal endothelium is the first tissue. The first objective is a Virtual Cornea built from spatial molecular data and clinical information.

Next

Other anterior segment diseases

Apply the same Discovery and Validation cycle to further corneal and anterior segment indications.

Long-term vision

The Virtual Eye

Extend the model to other ocular structures and ophthalmic indications.
